Privacy Notice

ClassScribe is operated by Edmita Investment LLC. Effective date: October 6, 2026.

This notice describes how Edmita Investment LLC (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use ClassScribe. It applies to our website, mobile applications, and related services. ClassScribe is a study aid, not a school’s student information system. If your school or instructor directs your use, ask them what separate school policies apply.

Important: ClassScribe is intended for people age 13 and older. We block account creation for children under 13. The product does not currently offer an under-13 school/education program or a school agreement that authorizes such use. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Information we handle

  • Account and age: sign-in identifiers and profile details you provide, an age-band declaration (13–17 or 18+), account preferences, and identifiers used to connect your account to a private library.
  • Study library: courses, schedules, lecture titles and dates, recordings, transcripts, notes, uploaded course materials, study guides, questions, AI-generated responses, and related learning activity you choose to save.
  • Recording consent: when applicable, the recording session and course/lecture identifiers, the consent-notice version and exact text, platform and app version, whether an accommodation was declared, and the consent time. Do not record a class unless you have the required permissions.
  • Billing: plan and subscription status, purchase and credit transactions, and billing-provider identifiers. Payment-card details are handled by the applicable payment provider, not stored in your study library.
  • Technical and support data: device/browser and application information, service diagnostics, security and access logs, and information you submit to support.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure your library; authenticate you; synchronize your data across your devices; record, transcribe, search, and organize study material; provide AI-assisted study features you request; manage subscriptions and credits; respond to support requests; prevent abuse; troubleshoot reliability; and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Before AI or cloud transcription requests, ClassScribe presents a separate sharing notice and asks you to explicitly allow processing for your library. Study requests may send your submitted questions, notes, transcripts, selected course materials, images, and relevant study context to OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude). Cloud transcription sends submitted audio to OpenAI or ElevenLabs. Requests pass through ClassScribe servers and Replit integration infrastructure. Device speech recognition may use Apple or Google speech services depending on your device and settings.

You can decline without deleting your library: existing library content, manual notes and courses remain available. You can withdraw permission in Privacy controls or the AI-sharing permission control in Account. Withdrawal prevents new AI and transcription requests; it cannot recall information already processed or undo an in-progress request. We keep the notice version, its text, your choice and the update time as a private library-scoped permission record. Recording permission is separate and still required for each class session.

Avoid submitting information you do not have permission to share. Providers process submitted information to perform the requested feature under the applicable service arrangements and their privacy notices. ClassScribe remains responsible for selecting provider arrangements and settings that protect personal data to at least the standard described in this notice and required by applicable law. Provider notices do not replace that responsibility.

Who may process information

We use service providers to operate ClassScribe. The application source and service integrations identify the following provider products or pathways; availability and routing can depend on the enabled feature and deployment configuration:

  • Clerk: user authentication and sign-in identity.
  • Replit and Google Cloud: application hosting/integration infrastructure and Google Cloud Storage for stored files. Replit connectors are used for certain connected services.
  • OpenAI: AI study responses, generated study material, and transcription where that feature is routed to OpenAI.
  • Anthropic (Claude), through Replit AI Integrations: study responses, summaries, note cleanup, and generated study tools using the submitted material and relevant context.
  • Apple and Google: device speech recognition where used by your device and settings.
  • ElevenLabs: timed transcription when the ElevenLabs connector is configured and the feature is available.
  • Stripe and RevenueCat: billing, subscriptions, purchase/credit processing, or entitlement checks according to the purchase channel. The stores themselves may separately process in-app purchases.
  • Google Gmail through the Replit connector: delivery of support messages where the connected mail service is enabled.

We do not currently identify Resend or SendGrid as an application email transport. A provider listed above is not necessarily enabled in every deployment or used for every request. Provider-facing account settings govern directly managed integrations; a connector can be administered through Replit and may have separate provider terms. Those settings and contracts can change. We do not promise any particular provider’s retention, training, location, or deletion practice on its behalf.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose library content to other students by default. Sharing features are deliberate and limited to the chosen class/group context. A supported school or instructor role must not access a student’s private library merely because of that role; access requires an explicitly supported and authorized sharing path. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights and safety, or in a business transfer subject to applicable law.

Students, schools, and recording permission

Students ages 13–17 must use the service only with any parent/guardian, school, instructor, and institutional permissions required where they live and study. Age band is collected without asking for a date of birth. A school’s approval does not by itself grant permission to record other people. Before recording, obtain all permissions required by law, school rules, instructor directions, and participants; disclose recording as required; and respect requests not to be recorded.

An accommodation declaration in a consent record is an attestation, not a medical diagnosis or verification. It does not replace permission from a school or other participants. The generated study tools are not certified grading, assessment, or professional advice and may be incomplete or wrong; verify important information with course materials and instructors.

Retention and deletion

The current service retention policy targets deletion of inactive study-library data after 24 months, with a warning 30 days beforehand where contact and delivery are available. When a user requests library deletion, app data is intended to be removed immediately and external-provider cleanup may remain pending until it succeeds. A pending cleanup does not mean the provider has confirmed deletion.

  • Financial transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes, keyed to a hashed library identifier and stripped of study content.
  • Consent records are retained for the life of the corresponding library unless a legal requirement calls for longer retention. Deleting a lecture removes its corresponding consent records.
  • Private export files are intended to be downloadable for 24 hours; a new export is limited to one per 24 hours. Deleting a library revokes its pending downloads.
  • Replit documents deployment-log retention of 30 days and production database recovery backups retained for 7–28 days, depending on plan. These documented periods do not establish when separate object-storage objects or checkpoints expire.

For details, see Replit's deployment monitoring documentation and Replit's data recovery documentation. Those platform periods do not confirm deletion from object storage or checkpoints. Their lifecycle must be verified separately and configured to a maximum 30-day target. Provider cleanup and data in retained backups may outlast removal from the live service. The published targets are not proof that every deployment has completed the required scheduler, provider, logging, or storage lifecycle configuration; we do not claim platform backups have already been deleted or a provider has confirmed deletion. Data may be retained longer where law requires or permits, to resolve disputes, or to protect service security. Legal review of this notice and confirmation of operational controls are still required.
