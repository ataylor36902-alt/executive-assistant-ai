# ClassScribe privacy operations: owner follow-up

This checklist records work that cannot be proven by the application source alone. Do not tell users that backups, provider copies, or logs are deleted until the corresponding deployed controls have been configured and tested. Legal review of the public Privacy Notice and Terms of Service is still required.

## Before launch and after provider changes

- Confirm the current deployment's provider accounts, enabled features, data regions, retention settings, and contracts/DPAs. Application source currently identifies Clerk (authentication), Replit (hosting/integration connector path), Google Cloud Storage (objects), OpenAI (AI and some transcription routes), ElevenLabs through a Replit connector when connected (timed transcription), Stripe and RevenueCat (billing/subscriptions/credits), and Gmail through the Replit connector for support email. Verify which integrations are enabled in each production environment. Do not claim Resend or SendGrid is used unless configured and verified.
- Review each provider's own privacy, retention, training, subprocessor, deletion, and data-location settings directly in its administrative console; record the console/account owner and review date in the operational system. Review whether each provider offers a contractual DPA and obtain it where required. Replit-managed connectors may require both Replit configuration and review of the downstream provider's terms.
- Ensure the current standalone pages `/privacy.html` and `/terms.html` are published from `artifacts/classscribe/web-src/static/` through `pnpm --filter @workspace/classscribe run build:web:source`, included in the public static build, and reachable at the canonical site root. Confirm the production mobile build sets `EXPO_PUBLIC_WEB_URL` or `EXPO_PUBLIC_DOMAIN` to the canonical web origin.
- Have qualified privacy counsel review jurisdiction-specific terms, age requirements, school/education use, recording consent, disclosures, retention, and the full processor/subprocessor arrangements before public launch. These pages do not by themselves establish COPPA, FERPA, GDPR, or other legal compliance.

## Retention and deletion controls that require owner verification

- Wire the configured inactive-library retention job into a real nightly scheduler. Confirm its 24-month cutoff and the 30-day advance warning path, including delivery behavior when email/contact details are absent. Monitor failed and retried jobs without logging transcript, recording, or other study content.
- Replit documents deployment-log retention of 30 days and production database recovery backups of 7–28 days depending on plan ([deployment monitoring](https://docs.replit.com/features/publishing/monitoring-a-deployment), [data recovery](https://docs.replit.com/features/data-and-storage/data-recovery)). Confirm the active plan and deployed behavior. Those documented database backup periods do not establish object-storage or checkpoint expiry. Configure and verify object-storage/checkpoint lifecycle to a maximum 30-day target, record the actual provider lifecycle and maximum expiration lag, and ensure restores re-apply deletion tombstones. Live library deletion does **not** delete a retained backup immediately. Do not claim that object storage/checkpoints or platform backups have been deleted until verified.
- Set and verify application, access, support, and downstream observability log retention to no more than the 30-day policy target. Replit documents deployment logs retained for 30 days, but confirm the deployed service and any connected log sinks separately. Confirm no study content or export tokens are logged and review vendor-side copies and retention.
- Exercise external-provider cleanup for deletion requests in isolated disposable test libraries/test accounts only. Verify retry/resume behavior, what records remain, and how pending cleanup is surfaced. Do not use a real student library for destructive tests. Provider-side deletion confirmation must be distinguished from a request merely sent.
- RevenueCat external cleanup is currently blocked: the configured server-key request returns HTTP 401 and the connected OAuth integration lacks customer/purchase write scope. Obtain and configure a correctly scoped server-side key (customer information and purchases read/write), then verify authorization and deletion against a disposable test account. Until then, keep RevenueCat cleanup visibly pending; do not claim the live RevenueCat customer was deleted or silently treat an attempted request as provider confirmation.
- Confirm the seven-year financial record retention store uses a keyed hash of the library identifier and strips transcripts, notes, recordings, generated study content, and credit-action response payloads. Ensure webhook and billing diagnostics do not retain study content.
- Verify private export ZIP storage, opaque-token authorization to the owning library, 24-hour expiry, one-export-per-24-hour limit, and immediate revocation on deletion. Check that URLs, access logs, analytics, exception reports, and support tooling never capture tokens or archive contents.
- Confirm that deleting a lecture removes its linked immutable consent records as specified, while retained consent records for other lectures remain attached only to their original library.

## Operational review cadence

Assign named owners for nightly retention scheduling, storage lifecycle/backup policy, platform and application logs, provider cleanup, and legal/subprocessor inventory. Re-check the actual deployed configuration at least quarterly and after changes to hosting, storage, AI, transcription, billing, email, or analytics providers. Keep audit evidence private and free of student study content.
